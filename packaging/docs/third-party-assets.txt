Finbolt — third-party assets
============================

FONTS
-----
Plus Jakarta Sans   SIL Open Font License 1.1   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans
Inter               SIL Open Font License 1.1   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Both load from Google Fonts at runtime. The OFL permits commercial use and
redistribution. If you would rather self-host them — faster, and no third-party
request — download the families, drop the .woff2 files into assets/fonts/,
replace the Google Fonts <link> with an @font-face block, and keep the OFL text
alongside them as the licence requires.

ICONS
-----
Every icon is hand-drawn inline SVG written for this template. There is no icon
font or third-party icon set, so there is nothing further to attribute and no
licence to pass on.

IMAGES
------
The template ships with no raster images. The illustrations are CSS gradients
and inline SVG. assets/img/ is left empty for your own artwork.

DEMO CONTENT
------------
All company names, statistics, quotes, job listings and blog posts are invented
placeholder copy. The partner names in the trusted-by strip are not real
companies. Nothing here is licensed from anyone, and nothing needs clearing —
but none of it is true, so replace it before launch.
