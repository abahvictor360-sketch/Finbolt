Finbolt — licensing overview
============================

Two products, two licences. This file explains which is which; the licences
themselves are the other files in this folder.

  EULA.txt      The commercial end user licence for the HTML template.
                >>> A TEMPLATE. Fill in every [BRACKETED] field and have a
                >>> lawyer review it before your first sale.

  GPL-2.0.txt   The GNU General Public License v2, which governs the WordPress
                theme. Verbatim, unmodified. It also ships inside the theme
                package as LICENSE.

THE HTML TEMPLATE
-----------------
Yours to license as you choose. EULA.txt is a conventional single-end-product
licence: the buyer may build one website, modify it freely, use it commercially
and hand it to a client, but may not resell or redistribute the template itself.

If you sell through a marketplace (ThemeForest, Creative Market and similar),
their Regular and Extended licence terms govern the sale instead. In that case
delete EULA.txt from the package rather than shipping a second, conflicting
licence.

THE WORDPRESS THEME
-------------------
GPL-2.0-or-later, and it has to be: a theme that calls WordPress functions is a
derivative work of WordPress. This is normal and expected for a commercial theme.
Paid GPL themes are sold every day.

What it means in practice:

  * You may charge for the files, for support, and for updates.
  * You may keep your trade mark, brand name and demo imagery out of the grant.
  * You may NOT stop a buyer redistributing the theme's PHP, CSS and JS.

If that last point is unacceptable to you, sell the HTML template only.

THIS REPOSITORY
---------------
The repository root now carries EULA.txt as its LICENSE, replacing the MIT
licence it shipped with. MIT let anyone redistribute and resell the code freely,
which worked directly against selling it.

If you would rather keep the source MIT-licensed for your own reasons, that is a
legitimate choice — but understand that it grants every reader the right to
resell what you are trying to sell.

THIRD-PARTY ASSETS
------------------
See third-party-assets.txt. Short version: two OFL-licensed Google fonts, and
nothing else — every icon is original inline SVG and there are no raster images.
