Finbolt — changelog
===================

1.0.0
-----
Initial release.

* Ten static pages on one shared design system: home, about, benefits,
  testimonials, career, blog, support, contact, login, register.
* WordPress theme with eight page templates, a real blog loop, archive,
  search, 404 and comment templates, three menu locations, a blog sidebar
  and a customizer panel for brand, colours, buttons and footer details.
* Staggered scroll reveals, counting figures, hero parallax, scroll progress
  bar and back-to-top, all disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
* Every text element meets WCAG AA contrast, measured against the rendered
  backdrop rather than the declared colour.
* Verified from 320px to 1920px: no sideways scroll, no clipped content, no
  undersized tap targets.
