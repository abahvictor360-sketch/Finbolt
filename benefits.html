Finbolt benefits

Built for the way money actually moves

Same-day settlement

Money collected before 6pm lands in your bank the same day, not in three working days. Weekends included.

Every payment method

Cards, bank transfers, USSD, wallets and direct debit — one integration, one dashboard, one reconciliation.

Eleven currencies

Invoice a client in their currency and settle in yours, at the mid-market rate with the fee shown up front.

Fraud screening on by default

Every transaction is scored before it clears. Suspicious ones are held for review instead of quietly going through.

Recurring billing

Set a schedule once. Finbolt raises the invoice, takes the payment and sends the receipt without being asked.

APIs your developers will like

Predictable REST endpoints, signed webhooks, and SDKs for Node, Python, PHP and Go. Test keys work instantly.

Fair pricing

One rate, shown up front

Finbolt does not hold your settlement to improve its own float. Money is swept to your account on the schedule you choose, and the fee is the same number you saw when you signed up.
